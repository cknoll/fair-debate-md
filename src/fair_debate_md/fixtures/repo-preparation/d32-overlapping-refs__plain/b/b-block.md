Taken together these four points are weaker than they look, because they are not four independent arguments. Cost, breakage, packaging and taste all follow from the same decision to buy the cheapest capsule machine on the market, and none of them says anything about capsule machines as such.

A better machine would fix three of the four, and the fourth is a matter of taste. What you are actually proposing is that we replace a bad appliance with a different category of appliance, and that step needs its own justification.
