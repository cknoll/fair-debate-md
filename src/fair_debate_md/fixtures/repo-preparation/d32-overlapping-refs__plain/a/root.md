# The office coffee machine should go

I would like to propose that we get rid of the capsule machine in the kitchen and switch to a kettle and a French press. The machine costs us around forty euros a month in capsules alone. It breaks roughly twice a year, and every repair leaves us without coffee for a week. The capsules are not recyclable in any practical sense, whatever the packaging says. And the coffee it produces is, to be blunt, not very good.

A kettle and a press would cost us maybe sixty euros once, take up less space, and be impossible to break in any interesting way. The obvious objection is that it takes three minutes longer in the morning. I think that is a price worth paying. If anyone disagrees, now would be the time to say so.
